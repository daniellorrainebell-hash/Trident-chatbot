# INOVAITIQ: 60-Second Reel Treatment (v2)

## The idea: every pulse of light is a customer
On the circuit board, the blue pulses are enquiries: calls, web forms, messages.
- **Without a system:** pulses flicker and die out before they reach anything. Those are lost jobs.
- **With INOVAITIQ:** every pulse is caught, routed and delivered.

The chip and board are the stage. **The story is the business owner's day.**

## The marketing psychology behind the edit
| Principle | How the edit uses it |
|---|---|
| **Pattern interrupt + question hook (0–2s)** | It opens with a direct question about the viewer's own day, not a logo. Viewers decide in about 1.5 seconds. |
| **Loss aversion** | A missed call stands for a missed job that went to a competitor. People react more strongly to losing something than to gaining it. |
| **Show the product, don't describe it** | One call is followed end to end in glass "phone notifications": answered → qualified → booked → CRM → text sent. Viewers see it working instead of reading claims. |
| **Familiar UI = instant understanding** | The cards look like real phone notifications, so viewers get it in under a second. |
| **Before and after with the same call** | The 14:32 voicemail from the hook is the same call that gets handled later, which pays off the opening. |
| **One credible proof, not a wall of stats** | A single labelled UK government statistic sets up "why now". It is never shown as INOVAITIQ's own results. |
| **Identity close** | "More time for the business. Less on the phone." This sells the outcome the owner wants, not AI. |
| **Low-friction call to action** | "Tell us your bottleneck." It's a conversation, not a purchase. |

## Visual language: premium, not sci-fi
- **Glass cards:** frosted glass, a hairline white edge, soft top highlight and a faint blue rim light picked up from the board. Think Apple or a Vision Pro interface, not Tron.
- **One accent colour:** brand blue, used only for things that are "alive" (pulses, ticks, the "AI" in the logo). Everything else is white and graphite.
- **Motion:** slow and weighted, with spring easing. Cards glide in with depth blur and ticks land on the beat. No glitches, scanlines, neon grids or holograms.
- **Type:** light and bold weights mixed in one line. The logo uses the wordmark itself; UI and body text use a clean geometric sans (stand-in shown: Manrope).
- **Footage:** realistic macro photography with shallow depth of field, where light behaves like it does on real hardware.
