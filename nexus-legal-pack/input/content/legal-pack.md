## READ THIS FIRST

I'm not a solicitor and this isn't legal advice. What follows is a structured first draft covering the documents a UK online course normally needs, written so that a solicitor can review and amend rather than start from a blank page. That makes their hour cheaper and your position stronger.

Two things carry real risk and need professional sign-off specifically:

1. **The cancellation position on digital content.** Get the checkout mechanics wrong and you may be obliged to refund buyers who have already consumed the course.
2. **The earnings disclaimer and your marketing.** You're selling a business course containing a real £12,000 case study. Any implication that a buyer will achieve similar is a claim you must be able to substantiate. This is an area UK regulators actively examine.

# DOCUMENT 2 — REFUND AND CANCELLATION POLICY

> **SOLICITOR REVIEW REQUIRED.** This document carries the highest commercial risk in the pack. The mechanics below must match what your checkout actually does.

## 1. Your statutory position

1.1 If you are a consumer in the UK, the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013 normally give you 14 days to cancel a contract for digital content bought online and receive a refund.

1.2 That right is lost for digital content supplied before the end of the 14 day period **only where** you have given express consent to the supply beginning immediately and acknowledged that you will lose the right to cancel.

1.3 Our checkout asks you to confirm both. If you do not give that consent, your access will begin after the 14 day period ends.

## 2. What this means in practice

2.1 **If you tick the immediate access box:** you get access straight away, and you give up the 14 day statutory cancellation right.

2.2 **If you do not tick it:** your 14 day right is preserved and access begins on day 15.

2.3 We record which option you chose and the time you chose it.

## 3. Our own guarantee

> Choose ONE of the following and delete the others. Whichever you choose must appear identically on the sales page, the checkout and here.

**Option A — No refunds beyond the statutory right.**
Beyond the statutory right described above, we do not offer refunds, because the materials are delivered in full on purchase.

**Option B — [NUMBER] day guarantee, unconditional.**
We offer a [NUMBER] day refund from purchase, no reason required. Email [EMAIL] within [NUMBER] days and we will refund in full and remove access.

**Option C — [NUMBER] day guarantee, conditional on completing the work.**
We offer a [NUMBER] day refund where you have completed [SPECIFIC REQUIREMENT, e.g. modules 1 to 5 and submitted the Action Steps] and it hasn't worked for you. Send us your completed work at [EMAIL] within [NUMBER] days.

## 4. How refunds are made

4.1 Refunds are made to the original payment method within 14 days of us accepting the request.

4.2 Access is removed when a refund is issued. Continued use of downloaded materials after a refund is a breach of the Course Licence Terms.

## 5. Payment plans

5.1 A refund under this policy refunds the instalments paid to date and cancels future instalments.

5.2 Cancelling a payment plan outside this policy does not remove your obligation to pay the remaining balance, unless we agree otherwise in writing.

## 6. Exceptions

6.1 We may decline a refund where we reasonably believe the request is fraudulent, where the account has been shared in breach of the licence terms, or where the materials have been redistributed.

## 7. Chargebacks

7.1 Please contact us before raising a chargeback. We will try to resolve it directly.

7.2 An account subject to a chargeback may have access suspended pending resolution.

Design will not fix these. They are mechanics.

2. A second tick box, unticked by default, for the terms: *"I have read and accept the Terms of Sale, Refund Policy, Course Licence Terms and Privacy Notice."*

4. A record stored against the order of which boxes were ticked and when.
5. An order confirmation email restating what was bought, the price, the cancellation position and the contact route.
6. Your legal entity name, address and contact details visible on the site.
7. A separate, optional marketing consent box. Never pre-ticked, never bundled with the terms box.
